# Valentte email rules

Permanent rules for every Valentte marketing email (newsletters, scenario sends, flows). Apply them when building, editing or reviewing any email, not only when asked.

## Subject line and preview text

- **Every subject line includes an emoji.** One emoji, usually at the end, chosen to fit the content or season (e.g. 🍂 for autumn, 🌿 for diffusers/fresh scents).
- **Sentence case for both subject line and preview text.** Capitalise only the first word and proper nouns/product names (e.g. "A different scent for every room", not "A Different Scent For Every Room").
- **Subject and preview text must not repeat each other.** The preview text adds a second hook (a code, a second offer, a benefit), it does not restate the subject's offer or price.
- Keep the hidden preheader `<div>` at the top of the HTML in sync with the `preheader` field, so the inbox preview shows one consistent line.

## Product copy

- Don't describe Lemongrass & Rosemary as "A 100% Essential Oil blend."
