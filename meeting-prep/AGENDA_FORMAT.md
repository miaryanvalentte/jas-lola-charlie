# Meeting prep pages: agenda at the top

Every weekly prep page under Notion › CRM Hub › CRM Meetings (Monday campaign
emails, Wednesday abandonment, Friday wider CRM) opens with a short agenda.
It should take under a minute to read and be enough to run the meeting on its
own. The full detail follows below a divider for reference.

## Shape

1. **Blue callout** (🗒️, `blue_bg`)
   - `Meeting agenda · <Day dd Mon> · <topic>, <reporting dates>`
   - `In one line:` one or two short sentences: total revenue and its change,
     plus the single biggest story.
2. **At a glance:** one line, each area with a 🟢/🔴/🔵 dot, revenue rounded
   to £x.xk and its % change, separated by ` · `.
   - Monday: AM · PM · Evening · **Total**
   - Wednesday: Site · Browse · Cart · Checkout · **Total**
   - Friday: Welcome · Post Purchase · First to Second · Direct Mail · main pop-up
3. **## 📋 Agenda**: 4–6 numbered items, most important first. Each item has:
   - a bold topic of 2–6 words
   - one plain sentence with no more than two numbers
   - `→ Decide: / Agree: / Confirm: / Ask:` and the question for the room

   Keep each item under about 30 words. Wednesday and Friday end with
   **Recap:** who does what, by when.
4. A divider, then *"Everything below is the full detail, for reference if
   needed."*

## Rules

- Use plain everyday words, with no shorthand (WoW, CR, CTR, pp, LW, IDs or
  sheet rows).
- Round the numbers, and don't use tables.
- Every agenda item must be backed up by the detail below it.
- If data is missing, say so in the "In one line" text.
