# Meeting prep pages: top-of-page format

Every weekly prep page under Notion › CRM Hub › CRM Meetings (Monday campaign
emails, Wednesday abandonment, Friday wider CRM) opens with a simple summary
that works as the meeting agenda. The full detail follows below a divider, for
reference. Mia laid out the template by hand on the Wednesday page
"21 Oct 2026 · Prep (Tue 13 – Mon 19 Oct)".

## Layout

1. **Two columns, 50/50**
   - Left: a blue callout (🗒️, `blue_bg`) with `Meeting agenda · <Day dd Mon> ·
     <topic>, <dates>`, then `In one line:` with total revenue, its change and
     the biggest story.
   - Right: a grey callout, `At a glance:`, with one line per flow (🟢/🔴,
     £x.xk, % change) and a bold total.
2. **One coloured callout per flow**, titled `<Flow> · £x.xk (±x%)`, with two
   bullets:
   - **Changes:** a brief, plain summary of the changes made, each with its
     date. These come mainly from the comments on the Valentte Growth Goals
     sheet (read via Google Drive `read_file_content` with `includeComments`,
     mapped to a flow by the comment's anchor row).
   - **Good to know:** anything else about that flow: the main number move, a
     test winner or a problem.
3. **## 🔜 Upcoming planned changes**: changes planned or proposed but not yet
   made.
4. **## 🚩 Anything else to flag**: issues, risks, data problems and questions
   for the room.
5. A divider, then *"Everything below is the full detail, for reference if
   needed."*

## Rules

- Use plain everyday words, with no shorthand (WoW, CR, CTR, pp, LW, IDs,
  node numbers or sheet rows).
- Round the numbers, and don't use tables in the top section.
- Everything at the top must be backed up by the detail below.
- The routines read the sheet's comments but never reply to or resolve them.
